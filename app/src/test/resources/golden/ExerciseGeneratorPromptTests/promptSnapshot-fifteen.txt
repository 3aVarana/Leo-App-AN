Write a reading comprehension exercise in English about volcanoes.
The passage is an informative text that explains real, well-known facts about volcanoes. It is not a story and has no main character.
The passage must be between 120 and 180 words long, about 150 words in 14 to 17 sentences.

The passage must give the clues but never state the answer. The wrong answers are conclusions the clues do not support.
